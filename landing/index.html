Step 1 of 4 · The Concept
Welcome to Reflow
A visual platform for building and automating data workflows.
Setting up data pipelines usually means writing repetitive boilerplate glue code or managing complex backend infrastructure. Reflow makes the entire process visual: you drag nodes onto an interactive canvas, connect them into a DAG (Directed Acyclic Graph), and run transformations directly in your browser.
💡 Pro-tip: Everything you see on the canvas represents a clean, repeatable data transformation.
